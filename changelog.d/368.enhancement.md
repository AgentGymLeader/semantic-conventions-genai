Add `gen_ai.agent.decision.id`, `gen_ai.agent.decision.outcome`, and `gen_ai.agent.governance.ref` attributes for correlating agent spans with a producer-owned governance decision (policy, review, approval, or execution-context evaluation) without carrying that record's payload in telemetry; `decision.id` and `governance.ref` are opt-in, with `decision.outcome` recommended when `decision.id` is set.
